What is AmbleBee?

AmbleBee is a little camera that hands you a mission: a date or adventure idea to go out and do. Pick a coffee shop you've never tried, hike to a waterfall, get the strangest doughnut on the menu. You take photos along the way, and when you come home the camera docks into the Hive, a picture frame that keeps your photos as memories of where you've been.

The camera is a personal project, built by hand and still in progress. The missions are here for everyone, with or without one. No points, no streaks, nothing to fail: just a reason to go.
