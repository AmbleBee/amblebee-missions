Every message gets read.

Two quick forms cover most things, and they're the fastest way to get something fixed or added.

Report a problem with a mission

Is a place closed or moved, or is something wrong or unsafe? The easiest way is to find the mission, open it, and tap "Report a problem" at the bottom. The form opens with the mission ID and name already filled in, so you only have to tell us what's wrong.

Don't have the mission open? Use this button instead and type the mission's name yourself.

Report a problem

Suggest a mission

Know a great date spot or adventure? It takes about five minutes, and it might end up in a pack.

Suggest a mission

Anything else

For anything the forms don't cover, like a question about the site or a request to change or remove your name on a mission, email staff@amblebee.com.

Have a GitHub account and don't mind posting in public? You can also open an issue. Please leave out private details.
