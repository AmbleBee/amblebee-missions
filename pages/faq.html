Questions and answers

The quick answers, before you write in

Using AmbleBee

What is AmbleBee?

Date ideas and adventures, which we call missions. Each one says what actually matters when you're deciding: how long it takes, how much effort it is, roughly what it costs, whether it's indoors or out, and the best time to go. Open a mission, and go.

Does it cost anything? Do I need an account?

No and no. The missions are free to use, with no accounts and no ads. Some places charge for entry or tickets, and each mission gives a rough idea of the cost: Free, Under $20, $20-60 or Over $60.

Where are the missions?

Missions come in packs, mostly by state, plus a pack you can do almost anywhere, at home or nearby. Put in your ZIP code or city to see the ones closest to you. See every mission.

How far away is a mission?

Distances are as the bee flies: a straight line, not the road. The drive is often longer, sometimes much longer, so check the route in Google or Apple Maps (linked on every mission) before you go. More in Safety.

Do you see my ZIP code or location?

No. Your ZIP code, city or location is used in your browser to work out distances and is never sent to us. The Privacy page has everything that's stored and where.

Can I do missions with kids?

Yes. Choose who's coming to see missions that fit, and look for missions with a kids' version: simpler steps and photo ideas for younger explorers. You know your kids best, so check the safety tips too.

A place is closed, or something on a mission is wrong. What do I do?

Press Report a problem on the mission. It fills in which mission it is, so it only takes a moment. You can report one here too.

Are you connected to the places in the missions?

No. Unless a mission says otherwise, AmbleBee has no connection to any business, park or place in it. We don't run them, aren't paid by them and can't speak for them. Their hours, prices, rules and service are up to them, so check with the place before you go.

Suggesting a mission

How do I suggest a place?

Go to Know a great spot? and either share the idea in your own words, or build the whole mission step by step with tips along the way. Both get read.

What does each status mean?

When you send something in, you get a private status link. Here's what you'll see there:

  • Received: we got it, and it's in line to be read.
  • In review: we're reading it.
  • Working on it: we're checking the place and the details, or turning your idea into a full mission. It isn't approved yet, and it can still end up as "Not this time."
  • We have a question: we need a little more from you. Answer on your status page.
  • Approved: it's in. We're doing a final check before it goes live.
  • It's live: it's a mission on AmbleBee now, with a link to it.
  • Not this time: it won't become a mission for now, and we'll say why.

Reports go Received, Checking, then Fixed or No change needed.

You asked me a question. How long do I have?

30 days. If you gave an email, we'll send one reminder halfway through. With no answer by then, we close it for now: a suggestion becomes "Not this time" and a report "No change needed." You're always welcome to send it in again.

Will my name be on the mission?

Only if you tick "Credit me." Names are kept as a first name and last initial, like Max W. Want it changed or taken off? Send us a message.

I lost my status link

Your status links are saved in the browser you used, under Your submissions on the status page. If you gave an email, the link is in the first email we sent. Without an email we have no way to send it again, so keep the link somewhere safe.

Messages, stats and your information

How do you reply to a message?

We reply on your message's private page and email you when we do. Please answer on that page, not by replying to the email, so it stays with your message. If we've asked you something and don't hear back in 30 days, the conversation closes by itself. Write again any time and it opens back up. All sorted? Press Close this conversation.

Why do you ask to count my visits?

It's optional, and off unless you say yes. If you do, Google Analytics tells us which packs and missions people open and roughly which cities and states they're in. Together with the places people suggest, that's how we decide where to write the next missions and which ones deserve a second look. We only ever see totals, never who you are, and saying no doesn't change anything on the site. The Privacy page has the details and lets you change your mind.

How long do you keep what I send? Can you delete it?

90 days after your suggestion or report is finished, your name and email are replaced with "AmbleBee user." Messages are deleted 90 days after they're wrapped up. To have something removed sooner, just ask. The details are on the Privacy page.

The camera and the Hive

Can I buy the AmbleBee camera or Hive?

Not yet. They're still being built, starting with the first ones for us. You can follow along on the bee trail. The missions are here for everyone right now.
